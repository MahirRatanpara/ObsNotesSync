
# High-Paying Product/MNC Employers with Remote/Hybrid Roles in India

Several global tech giants and large product companies have major R&D centers in Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune (and to a lesser extent Ahmedabad). For example, Microsoft (Hyderabad, Bengaluru, Noida), Google (Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Gurgaon, Mumbai), Amazon (Hyderabad, Bengaluru, Pune), Meta/Facebook (Gurgaon, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad), Oracle (Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune), Apple (Bengaluru, Hyderabad), Adobe (Noida, Bengaluru) and Salesforce (Hyderabad, Mumbai, Bengaluru) all have large Indian offices[upgrad.com](https://www.upgrad.com/blog/top-10-product-based-companies-in-india/#:~:text=Microsoft%201975%20,Facebook%2C%20Instagram%2C%20WhatsApp%2C%20Messenger%2C%20Oculus)[upgrad.com](https://www.upgrad.com/blog/top-10-product-based-companies-in-india/#:~:text=Meta%20,Mint%2C%20Mailchimp%2C%20Credit%20Karma%20Bengaluru). These firms generally pay at or above the ₹40–50 LPA range for mid-level SDEs, in line with FAANG-level packages[codingwithiitians.com](https://codingwithiitians.com/faang-salaries-in-india-2025-what-to-expect/#:~:text=,5%20Cr%E2%80%93%E2%82%B93%20Cr)[levels.fyi](https://www.levels.fyi/companies/google/salaries/software-engineer/locations/india#:~:text=Senior%20SWE). Many of them also offer hybrid or flexible work models. Key examples include:

- **Google (Bengaluru, Hyderabad, etc.)** – Google India follows a hybrid model (typically **3 days in office, 2 days WFH per week**). Employees report a set schedule of in-office days (often Tuesday–Thursday)[hubblehq.com](https://hubblehq.com/blog/famous-companies-workplace-strategies#:~:text=Google%20has%20long%20supported%20a,cut)[timesofindia.indiatimes.com](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/technology/tech-news/google-has-a-new-work-from-home-rule-employees-cant-use/articleshow/124551383.cms#:~:text=The%20WFA%20policy%20is%20separate,legal%20and%20financial). Google pays very competitively: for instance, a Senior Software Engineer (L5, ~6+ years’ experience) has median total comp ~₹9.9 million (~₹99 LPA) in India[levels.fyi](https://www.levels.fyi/companies/google/salaries/software-engineer/locations/india#:~:text=Senior%20SWE). Google’s India pay ranges (from ~₹3.9M for entry-level up to ₹14.8M for high levels) are among the highest in the country[levels.fyi](https://www.levels.fyi/companies/google/salaries/software-engineer/locations/india#:~:text=Senior%20SWE).
    
- **Microsoft (Hyderabad, Bengaluru, Noida)** – Microsoft India generally allows hybrid work. Many teams operate on a “3 days in office per week” policy, with flexible WFH arrangements approved by managers[glassdoor.co.in](https://www.glassdoor.co.in/Benefits/Microsoft-Work-From-Home-India-BNFT155_E1651_N115.htm#:~:text=5)[glassdoor.co.in](https://www.glassdoor.co.in/Benefits/Microsoft-Work-From-Home-India-BNFT155_E1651_N115.htm#:~:text=Current). Glassdoor reviews note that WFH is available “depending on team” and that a **3-day office policy** is common[glassdoor.co.in](https://www.glassdoor.co.in/Benefits/Microsoft-Work-From-Home-India-BNFT155_E1651_N115.htm#:~:text=5)[glassdoor.co.in](https://www.glassdoor.co.in/Benefits/Microsoft-Work-From-Home-India-BNFT155_E1651_N115.htm#:~:text=Current). Microsoft’s compensation for mid-level SDE2 (around 3–5 years’ experience) typically falls in the high 40s–70s LPA when total comp is considered, reflecting FAANG-like packages[codingwithiitians.com](https://codingwithiitians.com/faang-salaries-in-india-2025-what-to-expect/#:~:text=,5%20Cr%E2%80%93%E2%82%B93%20Cr)[levels.fyi](https://www.levels.fyi/companies/google/salaries/software-engineer/locations/india#:~:text=Senior%20SWE).
    
- **Meta (Facebook, Pune/Gurgaon/Mumbai/Bengaluru/Hyderabad)** – Meta India offers very flexible work options. The company allows many engineers to choose **fully remote or hybrid schedules** depending on team needs[wfa.team](https://wfa.team/faq/does-meta-allow-remote-work/#:~:text=Meta%20supports%20remote%20work%20across,schedules%2C%20depending%20on%20team%20needs). In fact, Meta states that **“many employees” can work from home full-time** or adopt hybrid schedules[wfa.team](https://wfa.team/faq/does-meta-allow-remote-work/#:~:text=Meta%20supports%20remote%20work%20across,schedules%2C%20depending%20on%20team%20needs). (India offices exist in Hyderabad and elsewhere[upgrad.com](https://www.upgrad.com/blog/top-10-product-based-companies-in-india/#:~:text=Meta%20,Mint%2C%20Mailchimp%2C%20Credit%20Karma%20Bengaluru).) Compensation at Meta is among the highest in India; mid-level engineers (SDE2/SDE3) often see total packages well above ₹50 LPA, with senior/architect levels reaching crores[codingwithiitians.com](https://codingwithiitians.com/faang-salaries-in-india-2025-what-to-expect/#:~:text=,5%20Cr%E2%80%93%E2%82%B93%20Cr).
    
- **Apple (Bengaluru, Hyderabad)** – Apple India has major offices in Bengaluru and Hyderabad[upgrad.com](https://www.upgrad.com/blog/top-10-product-based-companies-in-india/#:~:text=Apple%201976%20,Mint%2C%20Mailchimp%2C%20Credit%20Karma%20Bengaluru). Apple generally follows a hybrid model globally (roughly 2–3 days in office per week). The company pays FAANG-level salaries; Apple India SDE2 pay is competitive with Google/Meta on market surveys, especially when stock grants are included[codingwithiitians.com](https://codingwithiitians.com/faang-salaries-in-india-2025-what-to-expect/#:~:text=,5%20Cr%E2%80%93%E2%82%B93%20Cr).
    
- **Salesforce (Hyderabad, Mumbai, Bengaluru)** – Salesforce’s India offices are in Hyderabad and other cities[upgrad.com](https://www.upgrad.com/blog/top-10-product-based-companies-in-india/#:~:text=Apple%201976%20,Mint%2C%20Mailchimp%2C%20Credit%20Karma%20Bengaluru). Salesforce globally announced a hybrid “work from anywhere” model post-2021. Employees typically come in a few days per week for collaboration. (Exact Indian policy isn’t public, but Salesforce is known to allow hybrid schedules.) Salesforce’s total compensation for senior engineers can approach the ₹40–80 LPA range, aided by stock grants, comparable to other cloud/SaaS leaders[codingwithiitians.com](https://codingwithiitians.com/faang-salaries-in-india-2025-what-to-expect/#:~:text=,5%20Cr%E2%80%93%E2%82%B93%20Cr).
    
- **Amazon (Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune)** – Amazon India has large engineering centers (e.g. Hyderabad, Bangalore, Pune)[upgrad.com](https://www.upgrad.com/blog/top-10-product-based-companies-in-india/#:~:text=Amazon%C2%A0%201994%20,Facebook%2C%20Instagram%2C%20WhatsApp%2C%20Messenger%2C%20Oculus). However, note that as of Jan 2025 Amazon **ended its hybrid policy**: CEO Andy Jassy announced that all corporate employees must be in-office _five days a week_ starting January 2, 2025[economictimes.indiatimes.com](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/new-updates/amazon-ends-work-from-home-asks-employees-to-return-to-office-full-time-like-pre-covid-era/articleshow/113413617.cms?from=mdr#:~:text=Amazon%20work,or%20approved%20remote%20work%20requests). (Exceptions are rare.) Amazon roles still pay very well – mid-level SDEs (3–5 yrs) in India see ₹18–28 LPA base with bonuses/RSUs often bringing total to ₹30–50 LPA[codingwithiitians.com](https://codingwithiitians.com/faang-salaries-in-india-2025-what-to-expect/#:~:text=According%20to%20a%202024%20salary,%E2%82%B975%20lakh%20based%20on%20experience) – but the lack of WFH may be a drawback given your preference.
    
- **Flipkart (Bengaluru)** – Flipkart (Walmart-owned) is India’s largest e-commerce product company, based in Bengaluru[upgrad.com](https://www.upgrad.com/blog/top-10-product-based-companies-in-india/#:~:text=Hyderabad%2C%20Mumbai%2C%20Bengaluru%2C%20Gurgaon%20Intuit,HQ). Historically it allowed hybrid work, but in April 2025 Flipkart “scrapped its remote work policy” and now requires **5 days a week in-office**[economictimes.indiatimes.com](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/technology/flipkart-ends-remote-work-policy-employees-asked-to-return-to-office-five-days-a-week/articleshow/120378830.cms?from=mdr#:~:text=Walmart,broader%20trend%20among%20tech%20firms). Compensation at Flipkart is high (SDE2/3 packages in the 40–70 LPA range are reported), but note the new full-time office mandate[economictimes.indiatimes.com](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/technology/flipkart-ends-remote-work-policy-employees-asked-to-return-to-office-five-days-a-week/articleshow/120378830.cms?from=mdr#:~:text=Walmart,broader%20trend%20among%20tech%20firms).
    
- **Atlassian (Pune)** – Atlassian is a software company (known for Jira, Confluence) with a big R&D center in Pune[atlassian.com](https://www.atlassian.com/company/careers/teamanywhere#:~:text=Atlassians%20are%20able%20to%20work,support%20from%20their%20leadership%20team). Atlassian’s “Team Anywhere” policy is fully remote-first: employees can work from _anywhere_ Atlassian has a legal presence[atlassian.com](https://www.atlassian.com/company/careers/teamanywhere#:~:text=Atlassians%20are%20able%20to%20work,support%20from%20their%20leadership%20team). In fact, ~78% of Atlassian employees work permanently from home[hirist.tech](https://www.hirist.tech/blog/top-10-it-companies-offering-permanent-work-from-home-2023/#:~:text=Remote%20Work%20Policy%3A%C2%A0Permanent%20Work%20from,Home%20%2B%20Hybrid%20Work%20Model). Compensation at Atlassian India is strong for engineers (often in the ₹40–60 LPA range for mid-level roles), with generous stock plans.
    
- **Cisco (Bengaluru)** – Cisco’s largest India engineering center is in Bengaluru. Cisco strongly supports hybrid/remote work. Glassdoor reviewers note that **“Hybrid WFH is highly encouraged”** and many teams allow indefinite WFH with manager approval[glassdoor.co.in](https://www.glassdoor.co.in/Benefits/Cisco-Work-From-Home-India-BNFT155_E1425_N115.htm#:~:text=Current)[glassdoor.co.in](https://www.glassdoor.co.in/Benefits/Cisco-Work-From-Home-India-BNFT155_E1425_N115.htm#:~:text=Anonymous%20Software%20Engineer). Indeed, employees report that as long as the manager approves, one can work from home (even indefinitely)[glassdoor.co.in](https://www.glassdoor.co.in/Benefits/Cisco-Work-From-Home-India-BNFT155_E1425_N115.htm#:~:text=Anonymous%20Software%20Engineer). Cisco’s pay and benefits are above average; competitive mid-level SDE2 packages (around ₹40+ LPA total) are typical, making it a lucrative option.
    
- **VMware (Pune, Bengaluru)** – VMware (now part of Broadcom) has major R&D offices in Pune and Bengaluru. The culture is very hybrid/remote-friendly: employees report needing **only 1–2 days in office** per week on average[glassdoor.co.in](https://www.glassdoor.co.in/Benefits/VMware-Work-From-Home-India-BNFT155_E12830_N115.htm#:~:text=Anonymous%20Member%20Of%20Technical%20Staff,III). Many say WFH is available with manager approval, and some units are effectively remote-first[glassdoor.co.in](https://www.glassdoor.co.in/Benefits/VMware-Work-From-Home-India-BNFT155_E12830_N115.htm#:~:text=Anonymous%20Member%20Of%20Technical%20Staff,III). VMware/ Broadcom’s engineering pay in India (for VMware roles) is competitive, often similar to Cisco or lower FAANG bands (mid-SDE totals in ₹40–60 LPA range).
    
- **Adobe (Bengaluru, Noida)** – Adobe has engineering centers in Bangalore and Noida[upgrad.com](https://www.upgrad.com/blog/top-10-product-based-companies-in-india/#:~:text=Amazon%C2%A0%201994%20,Facebook%2C%20Instagram%2C%20WhatsApp%2C%20Messenger%2C%20Oculus). Adobe has long had a formal hybrid policy (about **50% in-office** time) for its India teams[hubblehq.com](https://hubblehq.com/blog/famous-companies-workplace-strategies#:~:text=). That policy (announced in 2021) remains in place as of 2024: roughly two office days per week for many roles. Adobe pays well – typically ₹40–50 LPA+ total for mid-level positions – and offers good work-life balance under its hybrid model[hubblehq.com](https://hubblehq.com/blog/famous-companies-workplace-strategies#:~:text=).
    
- **Intuit (Bengaluru)** – Intuit (financial software) has a large campus in Bangalore[upgrad.com](https://www.upgrad.com/blog/top-10-product-based-companies-in-india/#:~:text=Hyderabad%2C%20Mumbai%2C%20Bengaluru%2C%20Gurgaon%20Intuit,Mint%2C%20Mailchimp%2C%20Credit%20Karma%20Bengaluru). Intuit India follows a hybrid model where **teams are expected onsite 3 days per week**[intuit.com](https://www.intuit.com/careers/hybrid-work/#:~:text=Our%20hybrid%20work%20approach%20is,for%20both%20collaboration%20and%20flexibility). Intuit explicitly embraces a “best of both worlds” approach: collaboration in office balanced with virtual work flexibility[intuit.com](https://www.intuit.com/careers/hybrid-work/#:~:text=Our%20hybrid%20work%20approach%20is,for%20both%20collaboration%20and%20flexibility). India engineer pay at Intuit is very competitive (mid-level SDE2 total comp often in the ₹40–60 LPA range), buoyed by high base salaries and stock.
    
- **SAP Labs (Bengaluru, Pune)** – SAP’s Indian R&D centers are in Bangalore and Pune. SAP uses a **flexible, trust-based hybrid model**. Employees may come in ~2–3 days a week; AmbitionBox reports ~82% of SAP India staff go to office 3 days/wk, while ~4% are fully remote[hirist.tech](https://www.hirist.tech/blog/top-10-it-companies-offering-permanent-work-from-home-2023/#:~:text=According%20to%20AmbitionBox%2C%204,office%20three%20days%20a%20week). SAP pays well – senior engineers can see ~₹40–50 LPA total – and often grants stock as part of compensation.
    
- **Salesforce (Hyderabad, Bengaluru, Mumbai)** – Salesforce India’s offices are in Hyderabad and Bengaluru[upgrad.com](https://www.upgrad.com/blog/top-10-product-based-companies-in-india/#:~:text=Apple%201976%20,Mint%2C%20Mailchimp%2C%20Credit%20Karma%20Bengaluru). Salesforce globally follows a “flexibility by role” approach (some jobs are fully remote-eligible; others hybrid). Indian engineers typically work from office ~3 days/wk. Salesforce pays competitively (mid- SDE compensation often in the ₹40–70 LPA range including stocks) on par with other top SaaS firms[codingwithiitians.com](https://codingwithiitians.com/faang-salaries-in-india-2025-what-to-expect/#:~:text=,5%20Cr%E2%80%93%E2%82%B93%20Cr).
    

In summary, **the top product-focused MNCs** in India that meet your criteria (large size, FAANG-like pay, and hybrid/remote options) include Google, Microsoft, Meta, Apple, Salesforce, Adobe, Oracle, Intuit, Atlassian, Cisco, VMware and SAP, among others. Most of these have _hybrid_ schedules (typically 2–3 days in office) and pay at or above ₹40 LPA for SDE-II roles[codingwithiitians.com](https://codingwithiitians.com/faang-salaries-in-india-2025-what-to-expect/#:~:text=,5%20Cr%E2%80%93%E2%82%B93%20Cr)[levels.fyi](https://www.levels.fyi/companies/google/salaries/software-engineer/locations/india#:~:text=Senior%20SWE). (Note: Amazon India and Flipkart _also_ pay very high but have recently **mandated full-time office attendance**[economictimes.indiatimes.com](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/new-updates/amazon-ends-work-from-home-asks-employees-to-return-to-office-full-time-like-pre-covid-era/articleshow/113413617.cms?from=mdr#:~:text=Amazon%20work,or%20approved%20remote%20work%20requests)[economictimes.indiatimes.com](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/technology/flipkart-ends-remote-work-policy-employees-asked-to-return-to-office-five-days-a-week/articleshow/120378830.cms?from=mdr#:~:text=Walmart,broader%20trend%20among%20tech%20firms), so they may not fit a remote/hybrid preference.)

 

**Locations:** Bengaluru and Hyderabad have the richest concentration of these companies’ tech roles. Pune also hosts major R&D centers (e.g. Adobe, SAP, VMware, Atlassian). Ahmedabad currently has far fewer large product/MNC tech offices, so you may find more opportunities by focusing on Bangalore, Hyderabad or Pune. Each listed company above has known campuses in those cities[upgrad.com](https://www.upgrad.com/blog/top-10-product-based-companies-in-india/#:~:text=Microsoft%201975%20,Facebook%2C%20Instagram%2C%20WhatsApp%2C%20Messenger%2C%20Oculus).

 

**Salary Note:** FAANG-like MNCs pay strongly in India. Industry data suggest mid-level SDE-II (3+ years’ experience) at these companies often earn **₹40–80 LPA+** total comp[codingwithiitians.com](https://codingwithiitians.com/faang-salaries-in-india-2025-what-to-expect/#:~:text=,5%20Cr%E2%80%93%E2%82%B93%20Cr)[levels.fyi](https://www.levels.fyi/companies/google/salaries/software-engineer/locations/india#:~:text=Senior%20SWE), while senior/architect levels can cross ₹1 crore. For example, Google’s L5 (~6 yrs) median total comp is ~₹9.9 M[levels.fyi](https://www.levels.fyi/companies/google/salaries/software-engineer/locations/india#:~:text=Senior%20SWE), and mid-career Meta/Amazon engineers commonly see ₹40–50 LPA CTC+ in-hand[codingwithiitians.com](https://codingwithiitians.com/faang-salaries-in-india-2025-what-to-expect/#:~:text=,5%20Cr%E2%80%93%E2%82%B93%20Cr). These figures illustrate that targeting 40 LPA+ is realistic at such firms.
