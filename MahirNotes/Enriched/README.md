## 🏆 Interview Success Tips

### Before the Interview
- **Research the company** - understand their tech stack and challenges
- **Practice on a whiteboard** - many companies still use them
- **Time yourself** - most coding problems should take 20-30 minutes

### During the Interview
- **Think out loud** - explain your approach before coding
- **Ask clarifying questions** - don't assume requirements
- **Start with brute force** - then optimize step by step
- **Test your solution** - walk through examples and edge cases

### After the Interview
- **Send a thank you note** - reiterate your interest and key points
- **Reflect on areas to improve** - what would you do differently?
- **Keep practicing** - consistency is key to success
