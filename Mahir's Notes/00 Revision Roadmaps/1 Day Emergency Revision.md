# 1-Day Emergency Revision

> Interview tomorrow. 8 hours. Maximise recall, do not learn anything new.

## Ground Rules

- **No new topics.** Anything you don't already half-know will not stick.
- Read cheat sheets, not deep-dive notes.
- Solve problems you have already solved — you're rebuilding recall, not knowledge.
- Stop by 9 pm. Sleep beats one more problem.

## Schedule

### 08:00–10:00 — DSA Recall (2h)
1. [DSA Cheat Sheet](DSA%20Cheat%20Sheet.md) — 20 min, read twice
2. [Pattern Recognition Framework](Pattern%20Recognition%20Framework.md) — 20 min
3. **Classification drill, 40 min:** open 20 problems you've solved. Read only the statement. Say the pattern out loud. Do not code.
4. Code 3 problems from memory, 20 min each: one sliding window, one graph BFS/DFS, one DP

### 10:00–10:15 — Break

### 10:15–12:00 — Language and Concurrency (1h45)
1. [Java Concurrency Cheat Sheet](Java%20Concurrency%20Cheat%20Sheet.md) — 30 min
2. [Java Memory Model](Java%20Memory%20Model.md) — 20 min (volatile, DCL, happens-before)
3. [HashMap Internals](HashMap%20Internals.md) — 20 min
4. [Executors and Thread Pools](Executors%20and%20Thread%20Pools.md) — 20 min
5. Say aloud: why `volatile` isn't atomic; why DCL needs volatile; the pool submission algorithm

### 12:00–13:00 — Lunch, walk, no screens

### 13:00–15:00 — System Design (2h)
1. [HLD Cheat Sheet](HLD%20Cheat%20Sheet.md) — 30 min
2. [System Design Delivery Framework](System%20Design%20Delivery%20Framework.md) — 20 min
3. [CAP and PACELC](CAP%20and%20PACELC.md) — 15 min
4. [Caching](Caching.md) — 20 min
5. **Whiteboard drill, 35 min:** design a URL shortener out loud, on paper, timed at 30 minutes. Then read your own notes and list what you missed.

### 15:00–15:15 — Break

### 15:15–16:30 — LLD (1h15)
1. [LLD Cheat Sheet](LLD%20Cheat%20Sheet.md) — 20 min
2. [SOLID Principles](SOLID%20Principles.md) — 20 min
3. [Design Pattern Selection](Design%20Pattern%20Selection.md) — 20 min
4. Sketch a parking lot design on paper, 15 min

### 16:30–17:30 — Behavioural (1h)
- Rehearse 6 STAR stories **out loud**, 2 minutes each
- Have ready: biggest failure, conflict with a colleague, hardest technical problem, time you disagreed with a decision, time you had to learn fast, a project you're proud of
- Prepare 3 questions to ask the interviewer

### 17:30–18:30 — Logistics and Wind-Down
- Test your setup: camera, mic, IDE or editor, internet backup
- Reread the job description and the company's engineering blog
- Print or open your one-page cheat sheet for tomorrow
- Lay out clothes, set two alarms

### 18:30 onward — Stop
Eat properly. Walk. Sleep by 22:30.

## Morning Of

- 20 minutes: reread only the [DSA Cheat Sheet](DSA%20Cheat%20Sheet.md) traps section and the [HLD Cheat Sheet](HLD%20Cheat%20Sheet.md) numbers
- **Do not attempt a new problem.** Failing one an hour before will wreck your confidence.

## The Five Things That Matter Most

1. Clarify before you code or design
2. State constraint → complexity → pattern out loud
3. Test your code unprompted
4. Name your trade-offs
5. Take hints gracefully
